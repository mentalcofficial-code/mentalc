Privacy Policy

This page explains how Mentalc handles information when you use the mental math game, articles, and contact links.

Information stored on your device

Mentalc stores recent game records in your browser on your device. These records are used to show your recent results and are not sent to a Mentalc server.

Analytics and advertising

Mentalc may use Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services may use cookies, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, browser information, and similar technologies to measure traffic, prevent abuse, and show advertisements.

Third-party services

Contact forms are provided through Google Forms. When you open or submit a form, Google may process the information you enter according to Google's policies.

Cookie choices

You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may work differently if cookies or local storage are disabled.

If you have questions about this policy, please use the contact page.
