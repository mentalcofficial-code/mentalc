Polyole Puzzle Game

Polyole is a simple puzzle game where you fill a square board with different shaped pieces. Choose a board size and drag pieces into place.

Play Polyole

About

The goal is to fill the entire board with no gaps. It is a quick spatial puzzle for short sessions.

How To Play

Choose the board size, press Start, then drag pieces onto the board. Rotate or undo when needed.

Tips

Try placing large pieces and corner-fitting pieces first. Undo early when awkward gaps appear.
