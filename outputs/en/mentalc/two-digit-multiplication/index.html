How to multiply two-digit numbers mentally

For two-digit multiplication, the easiest mental math trick is to split each number into tens and ones. With 23 x 45, think of 23 as 20 + 3 and 45 as 40 + 5.

Step 1: split the numbers

Breaking numbers apart makes two-digit multiplication easier because each small product is simple to calculate in your head.

Step 2: calculate the four partial products

20 x 40 = 800
20 x 5 = 100
3 x 40 = 120
3 x 5 = 15

These partial products are the core of the distributive property. Instead of trying to hold the full calculation at once, you solve four easier pieces.

Step 3: add the results

Add 800 + 100 + 120 + 15 to get 1,035. This method works well for mental calculation practice because it keeps every step predictable.
